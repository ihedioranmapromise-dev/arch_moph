03 About

A quiet practice

I am Moph. I build software the way I build anything — slowly, with attention, measured twice.

Moph Echo Mirror came from a simple question — what would a mirror say if it could listen?

Building is a practice for me, spiritual as much as technical. Show up, work with your hands, make something honest. That is the whole of it.
